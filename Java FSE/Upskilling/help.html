Help & User Guide

About the Portal

The Local Community Event Portal allows residents to explore upcoming community events, register for activities, and stay informed about local programs.

How to Register

  1. Go to the Home page.
  2. Scroll to the Registration Form.
  3. Enter your Name and Email.
  4. Select an Event.
  5. Click the Register button.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is registration free?

Yes. Most community events are free of cost.

2. Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. You can cancel your registration before the event date.

3. Can I register for multiple events?

Yes. You may register for multiple events if seats are available.
